## Custom web craft

A small practice for websites that do things other websites can't.

We take on a handful of commissions each year, for people who see their site as more than a template and want to push the envelope of what a website can do. Custom visualizations, physics-based interaction, generative interfaces, spatial UIs, research demos, all built over content that stays plain markdown.

With [moss](https://github.com/Symbiosis-Lab/moss-releases), your writing lives in files you can read, edit, and version without the website. No CMS. No platform to outgrow.
