## moss

**Your files become your website. One click.** Publish text, audio, video, notebooks and interactive work from your computer to your own domain. Sync to newsletters and platforms. You keep every file.

Markdown · Obsidian · Jupyter · HTML · Media

moss is free, open source and local-first. It works on your computer, with or without us.

## seta

The service behind moss.

- **Sign-in** By email link or a cryptographic key. No passwords.
- **Hosting** Publish to yourname.mosspub.com. Large sites upload in chunks.
- **Domains** Search for and buy your own. DNS and CDN are set up for you.
- **Payments** Pay for hosting and domains by card.
- **Newsletters** Readers confirm twice (double opt-in) before anything is sent.
- **Comments** Spam is filtered before you see it.
- **Beta logs** Send a log from the app when something breaks.

## Custom web craft

A small practice for websites that do things other websites can't.

We take on a handful of commissions each year, for people who see their site as more than a template. Custom visualizations, physics-based interaction, generative interfaces, spatial UIs, research demos, all built over content that stays plain markdown.

With moss, your writing lives in files you can read, edit and version without the website. No CMS. No platform to outgrow.
